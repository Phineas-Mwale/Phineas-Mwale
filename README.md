### Drop2Earn Developer | Full-stack Developer | Building technology with purpose

I am a developer from Zambia who enjoys turning practical ideas into useful, reliable digital products.

My current focus is **Drop2Earn** — a recycling value-chain platform that connects collectors, collection points, verifiers, administrators and recyclers in one coordinated workflow.

## Featured project

### [Drop2Earn](https://github.com/Phineas-Mwale/Drop2Earn)

A Zambia-focused recycling platform that helps make collection, verification, reservations, handoffs and payouts more visible and accountable.

- **Live application:** [drop2-earn.vercel.app](https://drop2-earn.vercel.app)
- **Repository:** [github.com/Phineas-Mwale/Drop2Earn](https://github.com/Phineas-Mwale/Drop2Earn)
- Role-based workflows for collectors, verifiers, recyclers and administrators
- Secure Supabase-backed data and authentication
- Responsive interface with light and dark themes
- Admin management, reporting and Excel exports
